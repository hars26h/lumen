## Lumen — Smart Campus Energy & Renewable Credits Ledger

Lumen is an automated, high-fidelity accounting platform engineered to monitor smart campus infrastructure, log real-time energy production and consumption data,
and manage the lifecycle of Renewable Energy Credits (RECs). By establishing a verifiable digital ledger, Lumen transforms how institutional campuses track carbon offsets, 
optimize microgrid distribution, and audit clean energy compliance. 

## Key Capabilities

* Automated Data Ingestion: Integrates seamlessly with campus IoT hardware, building automation platforms, and utility smart meters to fetch micro-level consumption metrics automatically.
* REC Lifecycle Management: Mints, tracks, and safely retires Renewable Energy Credits based on verified on-site generation (solar arrays, wind turbines, and co-generation facilities).
* Transparent Ledger Architecture: Provides an immutable, audit-ready data stream to eliminate double-counting of clean energy distributions and satisfy compliance reporting guidelines.
* Microgrid Optimization Analytics: Generates actionable performance reports outlining grid performance economics, energy losses, and optimal storage utilization.
